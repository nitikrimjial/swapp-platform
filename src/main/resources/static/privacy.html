Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-21

The short version. We collect only what Swapp needs to work: your account, your listings and your conversations. We don't sell your data, show ads or use tracking or analytics cookies. Your exact location is never shown to other members. You can download your data or delete your account at any time from Settings.

1. Who we are

Swapp is a community platform for exchanging, donating and giving away everyday items without money. In this policy, "Swapp", "we" and "us" mean the team that runs this website. For your personal data we act as the Data Fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). You can contact us at our support email address.

3. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal data, and we don't share it for advertising.
  • We don't use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies, and we don't build profiles of you for marketing.
  • We don't read your messages, except when a member reports a message to our moderators, or when the law requires it.

4. Cookies and local storage

We use one essential cookie, swapp_token, to keep you signed in. It is HttpOnly (scripts can't read it), limited to this site, and expires after 7 days or when you sign out. If you use "Continue with Google", two more essential cookies exist briefly: swapp_oauth secures the round trip to Google and is deleted when you come back, and swapp_signup holds your pending sign-up for up to 15 minutes while you accept these terms. We also remember your light/dark theme choice in your browser's local storage. Neither is used for tracking, so no cookie banner is needed.

5. Who we share data with

We use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf, under their own security and privacy commitments:

  • Render (application hosting) and Neon (database hosting), both in the Singapore region.
  • Brevo (sending account emails such as verification and password reset).
  • Google, only if you choose "Continue with Google": Google confirms who you are and sends us the details listed above. Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google account.
  • Google Fonts: pages load the site's typeface from Google, which receives your IP address when the font is downloaded.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim: only when you search for a place or use "near me", your browser sends that search, or your approximate position, directly to OpenStreetMap to find the place name.

We may disclose data when Indian law requires it, for example in response to a valid order from a court or government authority, or to protect members from fraud or harm.

6. Where your data is stored

Our servers and database are hosted in Singapore, and our email provider may process your email address in the European Union. Transfers outside India happen only as the DPDP Act permits.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account and content are kept while your account is open.
  • When you delete your account, we immediately erase your email, name, bio, location, photos, listings and notifications, and sign you out everywhere. Messages you sent stay visible to the people you sent them to, shown as from "Deleted member", because they are part of the other person's conversation too. Completed exchanges stay in the other member's history in the same anonymised way. Erased data may survive in database backups for a short period (normally a few days) until the backups are overwritten.
  • Verification and password reset links expire after 2 days and 1 hour respectively, and are deleted soon after.
  • Reports and moderation records are kept while needed to keep the community safe and to handle disputes.

8. How we protect your data

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes. Sign-in, password reset and other sensitive actions are rate limited. Email links are single-use and stored only as hashes. Data is encrypted at rest by our database provider. Access to production systems is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach affects your personal data, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of your data: download everything from Settings → Your data.
  • Correct and update your data: edit your profile and listings at any time.
  • Erase your data and withdraw consent: delete your account from Settings → Delete account.
  • Grievance redressal: contact us (below) about anything in this policy.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights for you in case of death or incapacity: email us.

For anything you can't do yourself in Settings, email our support email address. We may need to confirm it's really you before acting.

10. Children

Swapp is for adults. You must be 18 or older to create an account, and we don't knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has an account, please tell us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy materially, we will update the version above and let members know before the change takes effect.

12. Contact and grievances

For privacy questions, requests or complaints, contact our Grievance Officer at our support email address. We will acknowledge your message promptly and respond within the time the law requires. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
