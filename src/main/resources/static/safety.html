Safety tips

Most exchanges on Swapp go smoothly. These habits keep it that way.

In immediate danger? Call 112 (India's emergency number) first. Then report the member on Swapp.

Spot a scam

  • Swapp never involves money. Anyone asking for a deposit, a "token amount", a delivery or courier fee, or a UPI or bank transfer is almost certainly trying to scam you.
  • Never share OTPs, bank details, passwords or photos of your ID.
  • Be wary of anyone who wants to move the conversation off Swapp straight away, pushes you to decide quickly, or refuses to meet in person.
  • Don't click links sent by strangers, especially ones asking you to "confirm" or "verify" something.
  • An offer that seems too good to be true usually is.

Meet safely

  • Meet in a busy public place during the day: a café, a mall entrance, or outside a metro station. Many police stations welcome exchanges in their lobby.
  • Tell a friend or family member where you're going and who you're meeting, or bring someone with you.
  • For large items you need to collect from a home, don't go alone, and keep the visit short.
  • Keep your conversations on Swapp until you've met. It keeps a record if anything goes wrong.
  • Trust your instincts. If something feels wrong, leave. You can cancel an exchange in the app at any time before confirming it.

Check before you swap

  • Inspect the item, and test electronics, before you hand yours over.
  • Only confirm the exchange in the app after both items have actually changed hands.
  • For children's items such as car seats, cots and helmets, check they haven't expired or been recalled.

Protect your privacy

  • Your exact location is never shown to other members. They see only an approximate distance. Share your full address only when you're ready to arrange a collection.
  • Photos you upload through the website have their hidden location data removed automatically. Still, avoid photos that show your house number, street or car number plate.

If something goes wrong

  • Report it. Use the Report button on the listing, the member's profile or the message. Reports are confidential: the member is never told who reported them.
  • Block them. Blocking stops all messages, offers and requests between you, in both directions.
  • For fraud, also file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in or call the national cyber crime helpline on 1930.
  • For anything else, email us at our support email address.
